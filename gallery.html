Why a gallery?

The converter itself needs a TikTok sticker's file URL, and the only reliable way to get that URL is dragging a sticker out of TikTok DMs in a desktop browser. That works great on a computer - but it leaves a lot of people stuck:

- You're on your phone and don't have a computer handy
- TikTok's app doesn't let you drag a sticker out the same way a browser does
- You just want a sticker someone else already converted, without doing the URL/paste/convert steps yourself

The gallery is the workaround: if someone's already converted the sticker you're after, it's just a search and a tap. No dragging, no URL, no computer required.

Everything here was uploaded voluntarily and goes through review before it's public. Every card has a report link if something looks wrong.
